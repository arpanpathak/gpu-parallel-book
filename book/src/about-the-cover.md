# About the cover

The cover shows the roofline model, the one picture this book keeps returning
to. The diagonal is the bandwidth ceiling: below it, a kernel is limited by the
bytes it moves. The flat line is the arithmetic roof: above it, a kernel is
limited by the FLOPs it performs. The marked point where the two lines meet is the ridge point. The gears around
it stand for the machine the book opens up, one chapter at a time.

Each chapter opens with a plate in the same style. Chapter 1 shows the roofline
roof and the Amdahl ceiling dashed above it.

| Chapter | Plate |
|---|---|
| 1 | The Mathematics of Parallelism: a roofline roof and the 1/f ceiling |

The plates are generated by `tools/art.py`.
